How it works.

Plain-language terms for playing, appearing on the leaderboard, and buying the sponsor spot.

The game

Is Hormuz Open? is a fictional, satirical arcade game. The course, ships and events are invented. The real-world status indicator summarises a public official report and is not navigation advice, a shipping service, or a statement about any real vessel or person. The game is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or go offline at any time.

Leaderboards

Ranked scores are verified by replaying your recorded inputs on the server. Names you submit are public to everyone who opens the game. Names that are abusive, impersonate someone, or contain contact details can be removed without notice. Tool-assisted play is not permitted and scores produced with it can be removed.

Sponsorship

One sponsor spot is shown on the game menu and results screen. Taking it is a one-time payment in US dollars that must exceed the current sponsor's payment by at least one dollar. There is no minimum display time and no guaranteed number of views: a higher confirmed payment replaces the placement at any moment, and being replaced does not entitle you to a refund. If a higher payment is processed before yours, yours is refunded in full. A disputed payment removes the placement until the dispute is resolved. Sponsor names and websites must be ones you are authorised to promote; deceptive, unlawful or unsafe placements can be hidden without refund. Payment processing is provided by Stripe.
