How it works.

Plain-language terms for playing, appearing on the leaderboard, and sponsoring a day.

The game

Is Hormuz Open? is a fictional, satirical arcade game. The course, ships and events are invented. The real-world status indicator summarises a public official report and is not navigation advice, a shipping service, or a statement about any real vessel or person. The game is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or go offline at any time.

Leaderboards

Ranked scores are verified by replaying your recorded inputs on the server. Names you submit are public to everyone who opens the game. Names that are abusive, impersonate someone, or contain contact details can be removed without notice. Tool-assisted play is not permitted and scores produced with it can be removed.

Sponsorship

Sponsorship is sold by the day or by the week at the fixed prices shown on the sponsor page, as a one-time payment in US dollars. During the booked days (00:00 to 24:00 UTC) the sponsor's name appears on the tankers in the game and, with a link, on the menu and results screens. Booking a date that has already started gives only the remainder of that day. Dates are held for a short time while you pay; if they are taken before your payment completes, the payment is refunded in full. There is no guaranteed number of players or views, and a booking that has started is not refundable except at our discretion. A disputed payment removes the placement until the dispute is resolved. Sponsor names and websites must be ones you are authorised to promote; deceptive, unlawful or unsafe placements can be hidden without refund. Tips are voluntary and non-refundable. Payment processing is provided by Stripe.
