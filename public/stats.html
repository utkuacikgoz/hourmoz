How counting works

Online means an anonymous browser with a visible game tab that checked in within the last 90 seconds. Multiple tabs in the same browser count once. This page refreshes every 30 seconds while visible. Counts are estimates, not verified people.

Games played counts starts, including replays. Unique players counts browsers that started a game. Visitors includes browsers that opened the game without playing. Clearing cookies or changing browsers counts as a new user. Finished games include wins and losses. Returning players played on at least two UTC days.

Traffic sources use fixed categories. Full referral URLs, query strings, emails and raw IP addresses are not stored in game analytics. Visit attribution begins with this release; older games have no source. The anonymous leaderboard cookie lasts up to one year. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable client analytics.

Reports cover 30 days. Subsequent visits and games delete older analytics records. Online records expire from counts after 90 seconds and are cleaned up on subsequent check-ins. Names submitted to the leaderboard are public.

Sponsor views require at least half the placement to be visible for one second. Views and clicks count once per sponsor per visit. Shares count a completed share-sheet action or copied link, not a confirmed social post. Client-reported events can be blocked or manipulated; these are not audited advertising figures. Sponsor bookings do not represent total revenue.
