How counting works

Players are counted by the anonymous browser cookie already used for the leaderboard. Repeat plays are starts beyond each player's first run in this 30-day window. Returning players played on at least two different UTC days. Changing browsers or clearing cookies counts as a new player.

Completed runs include wins and losses. Share actions count runs with a successful share-sheet action or copied challenge link; they do not confirm a social post. Each event is counted once per run. Client-reported events can be blocked or manipulated and are not audited advertising figures.

No emails, IP addresses, or browsing histories are stored by the game's analytics. Run records are kept for up to 30 days and cleaned up when new runs start. The anonymous cookie lasts up to one year. Names submitted to the leaderboard are public. This page exposes aggregate counts only.
