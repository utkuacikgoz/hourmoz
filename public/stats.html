How counting works

Visits count game page loads. Visitors count anonymous browsers; playing visits are visits with at least one run. Traffic sources use a fixed category from campaign tags or the referring site. Full referral URLs and campaign text are not stored. Visit tracking starts with this release; earlier runs have no source attribution.

Players are counted by the anonymous browser cookie already used for the leaderboard. Repeat plays are starts beyond each player's first run in this 30-day window. Returning players played on at least two different UTC days. Changing browsers or clearing cookies counts as a new player.

Completed runs include wins and losses. Share actions count runs with a successful share-sheet action or copied challenge link; they do not confirm a social post. Each event is counted once per run. Client-reported events can be blocked or manipulated and are not audited advertising figures.

Sponsor views require a placement to be at least half visible for one second. Views and clicks count once per page visit across menu and results placements. Click rate is clicks divided by views; fast clicks may happen before a view qualifies. Sponsor figures are client-reported, not audited ad impressions or revenue.

No emails, IP addresses, or browsing histories are stored by the game's analytics. Visit and run records expire from reporting after 30 days and are deleted on subsequent visits or run starts. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable analytics in the game client; the leaderboard still uses its browser cookie. The anonymous cookie lasts up to one year. Names submitted to the leaderboard are public. This page exposes aggregate counts only.
