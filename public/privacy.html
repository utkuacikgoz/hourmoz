PRIVACY

What we keep.

The game runs without accounts, ad networks or tracking pixels. This page lists everything it stores and for how long.

Playing

Your browser receives a random identifier in a cookie so that one best score per day can be listed and restart limits can be applied. It is a random value with no link to you, it lasts up to one year, and clearing cookies replaces it. Each ranked run stores its seed, your recorded inputs for verification, the resulting score, and a coarse trail if you share a ghost challenge. Runs are deleted after 30 days. Leaderboard names and scores are public and are kept.

Analytics

Visits are counted with a fixed traffic-source category (for example "reddit" or "direct"), never the full referring address or any query string. Presence check-ins record a timestamp per anonymous browser and expire within minutes. Sponsor views and clicks are counted once per visit. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable client analytics entirely. All analytics records are deleted after 30 days, and only aggregates are published on the stats page.

Network addresses

Rate limiting uses a keyed hash of your network address that changes every minute and is deleted within a few minutes. Raw addresses are not stored in the game's database.

Sponsorship payments

Checkout, receipts, card details and your email are handled by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. The game stores the sponsor name and website you enter, the booked dates, the amount, the payment status and Stripe's references for the session and payment so that refunds and disputes can be reconciled. Paid sponsor names and dates are public; amounts are not. Tips go through a Stripe payment page and are not recorded by the game.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The real-world status indicator is produced by the server reading the International Maritime Organization's public page; your browser never contacts it. Sponsor links open the sponsor's own site.
